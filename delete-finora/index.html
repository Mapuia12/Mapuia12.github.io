Delete Finora Account & Associated Data Finora by Zo Entertainment If you signed in to Finora with Google, you can delete your Finora login account and associated Finora cloud data. Deleting Finora does not delete your Google account. Delete directly in the App Open Finora. Open Google account & sync. Choose Delete cloud account & data. Confirm the deletion. Re-authenticate with the same Google account when prompted. The App then attempts to delete your Finora Firebase Authentication account, Finora cloud-sync records, and the local database associated with that signed-in Finora account on the current device. Request deletion without using the App If you cannot access the App, send an account-deletion request to Zo Entertainment: Email a Finora deletion request You may include the Google email address used to sign in to Finora so the account can be identified. Do not send your Google password, PIN, backup password, financial records, card details or other secrets. Data covered by the request A verified account-deletion request is intended to delete server-side Finora account and synchronization data associated with the identified Finora/Firebase account, subject to any data that must be retained for legitimate security, fraud-prevention or legal obligations. Copies that exist only outside Finora's cloud are not automatically deleted, including: a separate offline Finora database that was never attached to the account; offline copies remaining on another device; backups, CSV exports, printouts or shared files that you created; and information independently retained by third-party providers under their own policies. You can delete device-local Finora data by clearing the App's storage/data in Android settings or uninstalling Finora. Exported files must be deleted from the location where you saved them. Verification To protect users from unauthorized deletion, Zo Entertainment may ask you to verify control of the Google account associated with the Finora account before completing an external deletion request. Privacy note: Email only the minimum information needed to identify and verify the Finora account. Never send passwords or financial ledger content.
